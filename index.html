商务教我懂人性。在腾讯云做 ToB 直客销售，每天和不同行业的人聊——"懂客户到底在为什么买单"这件事，我还在日拱一卒地学习。

创业教我懂生意。开过剧本杀体验馆，办过考研辅导工作室，从获客、定价、交付到带团队，亲手把想法做成了现金流——让我知道生意是一单一单长出来的，不是 PPT 里长出来的。

法学教我讲逻辑。在西南政法读法律硕士，也在律所与车企处理过真实争议——法律训练的，不是会背法条，是会拆解一件复杂的事的因果链，然后清清楚楚地讲出来。

Business taught me people. Doing direct ToB sales at Tencent Cloud means talking with people from a different industry every day — and "truly understanding why a customer pays" is something I'm still learning, step by step.

Entrepreneurship taught me how business works. I ran a murder-mystery venue and a postgraduate-prep studio — from customer acquisition and pricing to delivery and leading a team, I turned ideas into cash flow with my own hands. It taught me that a business grows order by order, not out of slides.

Law taught me to reason. I'm pursuing an LLM at Southwest University of Political Science and Law, and I've handled real disputes at law firms and an EV company — legal training isn't about memorizing statutes; it's about breaking a complex matter into its chain of cause and effect, then explaining it clearly.
